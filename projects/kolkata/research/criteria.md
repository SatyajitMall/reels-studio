# Stage 4: song criteria (KOLKATA round 3, 2026-11-06)

**Director's choices (Stage 3):**
- **Story:** "A Bengali far from home": memory of home and longing to return.
- **Language:** Bengali.
- **Era:** any.
- **Matching:** **mood only** (pictures follow the feeling, not each line).

**Story spine:** the home the viewer left, remembered in 6 beats. Shots are from `research/SONG_RESEARCH_METHOD.md` Stage 2.
1. **Morning by the river:** boatmen, ferries, the ghat steps, Vidyasagar Setu, Howrah from the river.
2. **The roads we took:** scooter rides, the Howrah walkway, red heritage façades, a yellow taxi, the tram.
3. **Where we gathered:** College Street books, the Coffee House adda, chai on the boil, the Uttam Kumar mural.
4. **Evening, play:** Maidan football at dusk, St Paul's, Victoria at dusk.
5. **The festival that calls you back:** Pujo nights (the lal-paar dancers, dhakis, the lit gates, the Dashami ghat).
6. **The city lit at night, the ache to return:** Howrah at blue hour, the Academy at night, fade.

**What the song must do:**
- **Theme:**
  - longing / separation (biraha), memory of home, of old days, friends or the city, or of returning;
  - or a love song to Kolkata / Bengal sung from a distance;
  - not a romantic heartbreak that never touches place or home (v1's failure);
  - not a cheerful city catalogue (v2's failure).
- **Mood:** bittersweet, warm, tender; a lift of hope toward the end is good. Not tragic, not party.
- **Tempo / feel:** unhurried. 65–110 BPM, or a half-time feel, so a bar or a sung line lasts 2–4 s, matching our steady holds. Avoid a busy 140+ BPM full-time groove (v2: 1-bar cuts on handheld read as rushed).
- **Vocal density:** sparse to moderate, with room between lines.
- **Energy over ~45 s:** a quiet open → a gentle lift → a fuller payoff → a soft end (or a natural end of phrase). Needs **one continuous section** of 40–60 s (Instagram's picker takes one stretch).
- **Recognition:** ideally known to Bengalis at home and abroad, or so beautiful it needn't be.
- **Distinct from the reference:** not *Pyaasa*'s "Jaane Woh Kaise Log The"; no Hindi 1950s film-song stand-in for it.
- **Availability (gate):** in Instagram's audio library (the director checks in the app), or a licensed instrumental we can bake in.
