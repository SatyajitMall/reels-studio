## 4. What this means for the song

1. **The story is right for this audience.**
   - Bangalore (22.8 %) and Mumbai (2.3 %) suggest about a quarter of followers are Bengalis living away, the "Bengali far from home" spine.
   - Kolkata + Howrah (33 %) are the ones at home.
   - So the song should ache for home without excluding the third who are there.
2. **The audience's era is 2005–2020.** 86 % are 25–34, so their Bangla music is the band / indie era: Arnob, Anupam Roy, Coke Studio Bangla. That is why long-list 1's classics (1950s–80s) felt wrong, and it backs long-list 2.
3. **Discovery, then shares.**
   - 92 % of views come from non-followers.
   - The 244K reel was carried by shares (4.6K, more than its likes).
   - The reel should give a "send this to someone who left Kolkata" moment: the ache in the last 10 s, plus a caption that names it.
4. **Fresh vs. proven audio.**
   - Shantiniketan (151) and Boipoka (106) are almost unused: distinct, but their audio pages bring little discovery.
   - Chiltey Roud (12K), Mayabee (18K) and Nodir Kul (65K) are proven and still not saturated.
   - "Kolkata" (92K) is the crowded default.
5. **Language.** English is 80 %, so keep the title "Calcutta" and write the caption English-first; Bengali lyrics are fine, because matching is by mood.
6. **Mayabee.** It's by Blue Touch (Dhaka), not the 2025 Srabon Habib release we first found; sources disagree on the year (2022?). The title means "enchanting". The theme isn't described by any source and the band is pop-rock, so we have to listen for story and for a cinematic sound before it can be scored.
