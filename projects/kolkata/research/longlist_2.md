# Stage 5, second pass: Bengali indie / acoustic songs with a cinematic sound (KOLKATA round 3, 2026-11-06)

**Why a second pass.** The director rejected the first pass's top 8 (`longlist.md`) with "find better". Asked what was missing: **"More cinematic sound"**. Asked where to look: **"Bangla indie / acoustic"**. So this pass looks for Bengali-language songs whose *arrangement* carries the emotion (bansuri, esraj, shehnai, clarinet, piano, strings, slow builds, long instrumental passages) and whose theme touches longing, home, memory, distance or return. The story, beats and gate are unchanged (`criteria.md`).

**How to read this**
- Every row was checked against at least one source (links in the row; full list at the end). Anything we couldn't confirm was left out or moved to "Checked and set aside".
- **What it's about** is our own summary of what the sources say. No lyrics are quoted. Where no source describes the lyric, the row says so: listen before shortlisting.
- **Sound.** † marks a detail a source states. No source gives a BPM or says how much of the song is sung vs. instrumental, except where marked. Tempo, vocal density and the best 40–60 s section are measured in Stage 6 (`song_fit.py`).
- **IG library.** No source shows any of these used as Instagram library audio, so rows say *unknown* unless a source says otherwise. The director checks the top 6 in the app (the gate).
- **Beats:** 1 river morning · 2 the roads · 3 where we gathered · 4 evening play · 5 Pujo calls you back · 6 night, the ache to return.
- **Excluded:** everything in `longlist.md`'s table and top 8; "Amar Shohor"; anything from *Pyaasa*. Coke Studio Bangla's "Shondhatara" was used in another of the director's reels and appears here only as context.
- **Name clash:** #9 below, "Ghore Pherar Gaan" (2023 film title track), is a *different song* from Moheener Ghoraguli's "Ghore Pherar Gaan" (first pass #2).

## Ranked top 6 (cinematic sound weighted heavily)

Before scoring, the director checks these six in Instagram's audio picker (the gate).

1. **Chiltey Roud** (Coke Studio Bangla, 2022): the most cinematic arrangement found (bansuri, shehnai, esraj, piano, sax), with a yearning for someone to come back that plays as the ache of distance. Its river-wide sound suits beats 1 and 6. The risk is that it shares a show and sound world with "Shondhatara".
2. **Nodir Kul** (Coke Studio Bangla, 2023): a clarinet opening, flute, khol and real water sounds over 9:22, so there's plenty of instrumental room to lift a 45 s section. Made for our river footage. The theme is life's uncertainty rather than home, so the picture has to supply the hope.
3. **Shantiniketan** (Anupam Roy, 2026): written as homesickness for a remembered place, slow and dreamlike, acoustic folk. The closest theme match among the cinematic-sounding songs, if its vocal isn't too dense.
4. **Meghpeon** (*Grihapravesh*, 2025): restrained, meditative and melancholic, a message sent across distance by cloud. The film itself sets a Bengali from abroad in a Calcutta house at Pujo. Check that the separation reads as distance, not romance.
5. **Esho Amar Shohore** (Meghdol, 2018): a slow, nostalgic piano build, with an invitation to "my city" that suits the city remembered at night. The city is Dhaka and the intro is a voiceover, so pick a later section.
6. **Boipoka** (Anupam Roy, 2025): the closest theme match of all (a wanderer led home by childhood memory, and its own video cuts London to College Street), but bright rather than swelling. It's here for story fit, and could take beat 3 if paired with a fuller song.

**Next in line:**
- *Ghore Pherar Gaan* (2023 title track): a perfect film premise (Kalyani to London), but the sound is undescribed.
- *Holud Pakhi*: acoustic childhood nostalgia, known to every Bangla-rock listener.
- *Bari Jabo Bari Jacchi*: a song of going home, sound unknown.
- *Parabasi Chole Eso Ghore*: the perfect text, but no cinematic recording.
