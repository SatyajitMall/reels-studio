## Before you post (once)
1. Instagram → **Settings → Data usage and media quality → Upload at highest quality: ON.**
2. AirDrop `pujo-ki-asolei-sobar_IG.mp4` and `cover.jpg` to your phone and save both to Photos. (The earlier copies on your phone are the old version: use the new ones.)

## Post
1. Instagram → **+** → **Reel** → choose `pujo-ki-asolei-sobar_IG.mp4`. Don't trim it: the voice and the cuts are locked together.
2. **Music:** the song under his voice was taken out so it can be added licensed. Tap **Audio**, search **"Jaago" Lifafa**, keep the suggested part, then **Edit video** → tap the music track → **Volume ≈ 15**, **Fade → Fade out 3 s**; the video clip stays at **100**. Instagram credits the song itself.
3. Don't add Instagram's auto-captions or voice effects: the subtitles and the voice are already finished.
4. **Next → Edit cover → Add from camera roll →** `cover.jpg`. Check the grid preview.
5. Paste `caption.txt`. Hashtags can stay at the end or move to the first comment.
6. Optional: add the location; share to Facebook.
7. **Share.** Then watch it once on your phone with sound.
