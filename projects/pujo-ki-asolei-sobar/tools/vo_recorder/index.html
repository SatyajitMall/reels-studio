Voiceover recorder: Pujo ki Asolei Sobar?

Har take seedha project mein save hota hai (audio/vo_director/). Green SPEAK pe line bolo; ticks wale words pe cut hai.

Ready
Abhi koi take nahi.
  1. Headphones lagao, warna guide voice recording mein aa jaayegi. Headphones nahi hain toh "Guide voice" off karo.
  2. Mic: iPhone Microphone best hai (phone muh se ~20 cm). Warna MacBook mic, laptop ke paas baith ke.
  3. ● Record take dabao, guide shuru hoga. CLAP! pe ek taali, phir 3-2-1, phir lines. Take apne aap 44 s pe ruk jaayega.
  4. 2–3 takes karo. Galti ho toh rukna mat, agla take. Main best lines chun ke har line uske cut pe set kar dunga.
