## Before you post (once)
1. Instagram → **Settings → Data usage and media quality → Upload at highest quality: ON.**
2. AirDrop `uski-aankhein_IG.mp4` and `cover.jpg` to your phone and save both to Photos. **Delete the older copy from your phone first** (the v9c file).

## Post
1. Instagram → **+** → **Reel** → choose `uski-aankhein_IG.mp4`. Don't trim it: the blinks are timed to the song.
2. **Music: the reference reel's song, from its start.** Open the reference reel → tap its audio name → **Save** (or **Use audio**). Back in your draft, tap **Audio → Saved** and pick it. Keep the snippet at its very start (0:00), the same as the reference.
   - *Check:* the first blink and boom (7.9 s) land on the drop, and the second (16.0 s) on the next hit.
   - *Volume:* music 100, your clip's own audio (the booms) 100. If the booms disappear, lower the music to about 80.
3. **Edit cover → Add from camera roll →** `cover.jpg`. Check the grid preview.
4. Paste `caption.txt`.
5. **Advanced settings:**
   - **AI info label: ON.** The esraj player in the opening is AI-generated, and Meta requires the label for realistic generated content.
   - **Alt text:** paste the line below.
6. **Location:** the city, or the pandal of the clay face if you'd like to name it.
7. **Share.** Then watch it once on your phone with sound.
