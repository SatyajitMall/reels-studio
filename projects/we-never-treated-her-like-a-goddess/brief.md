## 1. The idea
- **Reel type:** `hybrid`. No one speaks in our footage; the **reference reel's VO** (English, 7 lines, 0.00–27.24 s) is the soundtrack and the timing source. Cuts land on clause onsets (CLAUDE.md §4b logic applied to an external VO, as in Pujo ki Asolei Sobar).
- **Playbook:** `story-montage` (this reel is its pilot). **Overrides**, each with its reason:
  1. **No sizzle hook.** The VO starts on frame 0 with the thesis line; the hook is that line over a slow push into the grandest goddess, a dark crowd raising phones before her (Oct 15 C089; round 2).
  2. **Ma is the recurring character** (on screen in nearly every shot: pandals, Dashami, the home thakurdalan). The boy on his father's shoulders (Oct 21 C042 / C044, same boy and father) appears at #3 "I grew up calling her Ma" and #8 "losing their daughter", the narrator as a child.
  3. **Subtitles in yellow sans like the reference** (director, Round 2), not the playbook's white serif.
  4. **Colour: `muted_red` at tolerance 45** so gold stays with red (director, Round 2); the playbook's 12–25 turns gold idols silver.
  5. **No music bed of our own:** the reference audio already carries VO + bed, so no ducking stage (§Sound).
- **One-line concept:** For four days a year Bengal treats its goddess like a daughter come home: we dress her, feed her, and cry when she leaves.
- **Story arc (Goddess → daughter, director; round 2 re-pick):** the grand goddess everyone films → people walking in to her → the boy on his father's shoulders → Dashami: hands garlanding her, Boron (women feeding and touching her face), carried out, a hand at her eye, the boy watching her lowered into the Ganga → the dhak at the farewell, women in lal-paar saris → "one of us": Boron, a woman at Ma's face → Ma on the truck, her eyes, the pandals, the Pujo street, the everyday city (yellow taxi, daytime) → home again in the thakurdalan → আসছে বছর আবার হবে.
- **Goal:** ache and reflect; the last line lands and stays. Saves and shares (sent to Ma, to family groups).
- **Audience:** Bengali diaspora away from home this Pujo.
- **Target length:** the VO: picture to 27.48 s + end card = **29.84 s** (reference audio 29.85 s as decoded; its container says 29.95 s).
